# Acme Corp - Business Travel Policy

## 1. Flight Bookings and Travel Classes
Acme Corp supports employees traveling for business purposes. To manage costs effectively:
- Economy class must be booked for all domestic flights and any flights with a duration under 6 hours.
- Business class is permitted only for international flights with a flight duration exceeding 6 continuous hours, subject to prior written approval from the department VP.
- All flight bookings must be made through the official corporate travel portal (AcmeTravel) at least 14 days in advance to secure optimal pricing.

## 2. Lodging and Hotel Accommodation
Employees are expected to choose comfortable but mid-range accommodations. The maximum allowable nightly rates for hotel rooms are:
- Metro Cities (e.g., Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore): up to **₹5,000 per night** (excluding taxes).
- Non-Metro Cities: up to **₹3,500 per night** (excluding taxes).
- Any bookings exceeding these limits must be justified and approved by the travel coordinator before booking.

## 3. Meal Allowance and Per Diem
Acme Corp provides a daily meal allowance (per diem) to cover breakfast, lunch, dinner, and incidental costs:
- For domestic travel within India, the daily meal allowance is **₹1,500 per day**.
- For international travel, the daily meal allowance is **$75 USD per day** (or equivalent in local currency).
- Receipts are not required for the standard per diem allowance, but employees must log their travel days accurately in the Travel Portal.

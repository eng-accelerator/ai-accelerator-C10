# Acme Corp - Work From Home (WFH) & Remote Work Policy

## 1. Overview and Eligibility
Acme Corp supports flexible working arrangements to help employees manage their personal and professional commitments. All full-time office-based employees who have completed their initial 3-month probation period are eligible to participate in the hybrid work scheme.

## 2. Hybrid Work Schedule
Eligible employees may work remotely **up to 2 days per week**. The remaining 3 days must be worked from the designated Acme Corp office. Core collaboration days are Tuesdays and Thursdays, during which all team members are strongly encouraged to be physically present in the office.

This arrangement is subject to business needs and may be modified or revoked by management if performance standards are not met or if job duties require on-site presence.

## 3. Communication and Notification
To maintain team coordination, employees should inform their manager before working remotely. Notification must be sent via email or Slack by 5:00 PM on the day prior to the remote work day.

While working from home, employees are expected to:
- Be reachable via standard communication channels (Slack, email, phone) during regular working hours (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM).
- Participate in all scheduled team meetings and stand-ups via video conferencing.
- Maintain a secure, distraction-free workspace with reliable, high-speed internet access.

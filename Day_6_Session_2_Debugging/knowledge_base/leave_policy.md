# Acme Corp - Employee Leave Policy

## 1. Annual Leave Allowance
At Acme Corp, we believe in work-life balance and encourage all employees to take regular time off to rest and recharge. All full-time employees are entitled to **24 days of annual leave** per calendar year. This leave is accrued monthly at a rate of 2 days per month of service.

Part-time employees accrue annual leave on a pro-rata basis based on their contracted hours of work.

## 2. Carry Forward Rules
Employees are encouraged to plan and utilize their annual leave during the year in which it is accrued. However, we understand that projects and personal circumstances can sometimes interfere. Therefore, a maximum of **5 unused annual leave days** can be carried forward into the next calendar year. Any carried forward leave must be used before March 31st of the new year, or it will be forfeited. No cash payouts are allowed for unused leave, except upon separation from the company.

## 3. Leave Application Process
To ensure smooth business operations, employees must plan their time off in consultation with their teams and managers.
- All leave requests should be submitted through the HR Portal at least **3 working days in advance** for leaves lasting up to 3 days.
- For longer absences (4 days or more), requests must be submitted at least **10 working days in advance**.
- Emergency leave or sick leave can be registered post facto upon return to work, along with proper medical certificates where applicable.
- Managers are required to review and approve or reject leave requests within 48 hours of submission.

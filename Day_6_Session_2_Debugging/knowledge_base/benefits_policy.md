# Acme Corp - Employee Benefits Policy

## 1. Health and Medical Insurance
Acme Corp provides comprehensive health coverage to protect employees and their families. All full-time employees are enrolled in the Group Health Insurance policy starting from their date of joining.
- The policy covers the employee, their spouse, and up to **2 dependent children**.
- The standard coverage limit (sum insured) is **₹5,00,000 per family** per policy year.
- Pre-existing diseases are covered from day one of enrollment. Maternity benefits are capped at ₹50,000 for normal delivery and ₹75,000 for caesarean section.

## 2. Wellness Reimbursement
To promote a healthy lifestyle, Acme Corp reimburses wellness-related activities:
- Employees can claim up to **₹1,500 per month** for gym memberships, yoga classes, sports club memberships, or mental health counseling services.
- Wellness claims must be submitted with valid payment receipts in the Benefits portal monthly.

## 3. Professional Development and Training
We encourage continuous learning. Acme Corp offers an annual professional development allowance:
- Employees receive up to **₹50,000 per calendar year** for approved courses, certifications, workshops, or academic textbooks related to their current role.
- Approval from the direct manager and HR is required prior to enrollment to guarantee reimbursement.

## 4. Work-from-Home Broadband Allowance
To support our hybrid model, Acme Corp provides a internet/broadband reimbursement of up to **₹1,000 per month** for eligible employees working remotely. Claims should be submitted under the "Internet/Broadband" category in the portal.

# Acme Corp - Business Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. General Principles
Acme Corp will reimburse employees for reasonable, necessary, and pre-approved business expenses incurred in the direct course of performing their job responsibilities. All expense claims must align with company values, be cost-conscious, and represent a clear business purpose.

## 2. Submission Deadlines
To ensure accurate accounting and financial reporting, business expenses must be submitted **within 30 days** of incurring the expense or returning from a business trip. Expenses submitted after this 30-day window will be rejected unless accompanied by written exception approval from the department VP.

## 3. Receipt Documentation Rules
Employees must provide proof of payment for all claims to be processed.
- Receipts are required for expenses **above ₹500** (Five Hundred Indian Rupees).
- For expenses ₹500 or below, a written explanation detailing the date, amount, vendor, and business purpose of the expense is acceptable if a formal receipt was unavailable.
- Digital copies or photos of receipts should be uploaded directly to the Expense Portal. Physical receipts must be retained by the employee for 6 months for auditing purposes.
- Split-billing to bypass the ₹500 receipt threshold is strictly prohibited and constitutes a policy violation.
